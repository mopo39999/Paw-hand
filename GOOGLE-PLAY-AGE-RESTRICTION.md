# Google Play age-restriction launch note

Paw & Hand is a dating app and is already coded as 18+:

- Signup requires an 18+ confirmation.
- Profile age cannot be below 18.
- Terms state the service is for adults only.

Google Play has announced an expansion of its Age-Restricted Content and Functionality policy effective August 26, 2026. Dating/matchmaking apps are among the covered app types and are required to use Play Console functionality/tools to block minors.

That final Play Console configuration is account-side, not source code, so it cannot be completed inside this repository. Complete it before publishing or updating the app after the policy takes effect.
