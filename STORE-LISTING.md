## Full description

Paw & Hand is dog-first dating for people who know a relationship is never just about two humans.

Build one profile for you and one for your dog. Paw & Hand looks at three different kinds of fit — People Compatibility, Dog Compatibility, and Lifestyle Compatibility — so a promising match makes sense on both ends of the leash.

### Match beyond a profile photo

Paw & Hand considers relationship goals, everyday preferences, shared activities, dog energy, dog size, social style, and the things you say matter most. You can adjust the weight of People, Dog, and Lifestyle compatibility instead of letting a black-box score decide everything for you.

### Dog Date Mode

When you match, suggest a public dog-friendly meetup right from the conversation. A walk, park, trail, or dog-friendly cafe can make a first meeting feel more natural while giving both dogs room to be part of the experience.

### Built around safety and control

Paw & Hand includes profile and conversation reporting, message-level reporting, blocking, baseline automated text filtering, private profile media, account visibility controls, and permanent account deletion. The app is for adults 18 and older.

### Lightweight by design

Paw & Hand uses a small mobile-first interface without ad SDKs or a heavy front-end framework. Photos are resized before upload, precise GPS is not required by the MVP, and the same codebase can run on web, Android, and iPhone.

Paw & Hand: find someone worth walking beside.
