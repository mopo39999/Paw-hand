PAW & HAND

Safety Center

Paw & Hand is for adults 18 and older. Dating and meeting new dogs both involve real-world judgment, so compatibility scores are never a substitute for your own safety decisions.

Before meeting

Keep early conversation in the app until you are comfortable. Do not send money, gift cards, crypto, banking credentials, passwords, or verification codes to a match.

First Dog Date

Choose a busy public place, tell someone where you are going, control your own transportation, and leave if anything feels wrong. For the dogs, start on neutral ground and respect each animal's body language, training, health, and comfort.

Reporting and blocking

Profiles and conversations include report and block controls. A report can reference the conversation or a specific message. Blocking removes the pair's access to the match through the app's database access rules.

Automated moderation

The service applies a baseline server-side text filter to profile text, chat messages, and Dog Date text, plus rate limits intended to reduce spam. Automated filtering is only one layer and cannot understand every context or detect every harmful image or coded phrase.

Emergencies

If you believe you or another person is in immediate physical danger, use the emergency services available where you are located. Paw & Hand reporting is not an emergency-response service.

Operator response

Safety reports, appeals, and support requests can also be sent to mopo39999@gmail.com. Before public launch, Paw & Hand must also assign human moderation ownership and an urgent-safety escalation process.
