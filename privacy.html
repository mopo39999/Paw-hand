PAW & HAND

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Paw & Hand is a dog-first dating service for adults 18 and older. This policy describes the data used by the current application.

Data we process

We process account email through Supabase Auth; profile information such as name, age, dating preferences, biography, lifestyle selections, approximate location label, and dog information; profile photos; swipe choices; matches; messages; Dog Date proposals; blocks; and safety reports.

How it is used

Data is used to authenticate accounts, show eligible profiles, calculate compatibility, create matches, deliver messages, support Dog Date proposals, and operate safety features. The current app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Location

The MVP does not require or store precise GPS coordinates. Users may provide an approximate location label for matching context.

Photos and security

Profile media is stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. The client receives expiring signed links to display private media. Database access is protected with Row Level Security.

Safety and moderation

Profile text, chat messages, and Dog Date text may be checked by baseline automated rules intended to reduce clearly prohibited content and spam. Safety reports may include the related profile, match, or message identifiers so the operator can investigate abuse. Automated filtering is not a substitute for human moderation.

Deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account from Profile → Account & privacy. An external deletion flow is also available at Delete account. Account deletion removes profile media before deleting the authentication account; app records tied to that account are deleted through database relationships.

Retention and legal requests

The production operator must document any legally required retention, fraud-prevention retention, backup behavior, and jurisdiction-specific privacy rights before public launch.
